# Smart TV Racer

A pixel-art racing game for smart TVs. Fixed third-person view, simple remote-friendly controls.

## Idea

Cars draft: sitting behind another car makes you faster. To take the lead you have to pass on the left or the right. That pass is where the fight happens.

The leader can smash the contender into the roadside. Both cars pay for it — the leader loses speed, the contender loses more, and may briefly stop. Durability wears down on hits; crossing a threshold forces a short stop.

The contender can juke. Brake a little as the smash comes in, and the leader misses and hits the barrier instead.

## Controls

Built around a TV remote: steer, brake, confirm. Nothing that needs a gamepad.
